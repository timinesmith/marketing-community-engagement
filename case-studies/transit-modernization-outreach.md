# Transit Modernization Community Outreach — Sanitized Case Study

## Context

A multi-year Philadelphia trolley-modernization program required sustained, in-person engagement so riders and communities could understand construction plans, service changes, and timelines.

## Timothy's role

Project Manager / Field Operations Supervisor with Community Marketing Concepts, supporting the program during the documented 2022 outreach season. His broader CMC engagement was project-based from 2005–2025.

## Work performed

- Supervised field outreach teams; the program consultant requested teams of 2–4 staff per deployment.
- Staffed and coordinated pop-ups at 13+ locations, including major stations, a transportation center, the Philadelphia Zoo, and neighborhood corridors.
- Supported festival outreach at West Philly PorchFest, Odunde Festival, Juneteenth at Malcolm X Park, and Upper Darby Pride.
- Sent the program summary and stakeholder presentation to Delaware County's DEI Officer.
- Coordinated a program briefing for Delaware County Council and arranged municipal pop-up and survey logistics.
- Produced outreach reporting, including a Bartram Village outreach report.
- Participated in recurring communication-team check-ins and consultant touchbases.

## Results

The work provided supervised, visible field coverage across transit and community settings, direct county-level stakeholder coordination, and documented reporting during the June–July 2022 outreach period.

## Evidence note

The role and deployments are corroborated by 2022 program email and Timothy's stakeholder workbooks. Those workbooks contain personal contact information and are intentionally excluded from this public portfolio.
