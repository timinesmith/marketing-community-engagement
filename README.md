# Marketing & Community Engagement

## Executive summary

A sanitized community-engagement portfolio anchored in Timothy Nesmith's verified work on a major Philadelphia trolley-modernization outreach program. It provides campaign planning, stakeholder communication, field activity, and reporting frameworks for public-facing initiatives.

## Business problem

Infrastructure and public-service programs need more than announcements. Residents and riders need timely information, accessible opportunities to ask questions, and confidence that feedback is recorded and routed to decision-makers.

## Scope

- Pop-up and community-event outreach
- Stakeholder communications
- Field-team deployment and reporting
- Feedback categorization
- Municipal and community-partner coordination

## Role and evidence basis

**Real case basis:** Project Manager / Field Operations Supervisor, Community Marketing Concepts, documented on the trolley-modernization program in 2022. Timothy supervised field teams of 2–4 per deployment, staffed pop-ups at 13+ locations and major festivals, coordinated county stakeholder activity, and produced outreach reporting.

**Demonstration status: Illustrative demonstration.** Real stakeholder call lists and contact information were used only to verify the work and are not included. Frameworks and sample logs in this repository are newly created demonstrations.

## Methods and tools

Audience segmentation, location planning, message discipline, field briefing packets, activity logs, feedback coding, stakeholder follow-up tracking, and weekly reporting.

## Verified outcomes

- Pop-up outreach delivered at 13+ named stations, transportation centers, and community locations.
- Festival outreach included West Philly PorchFest, Odunde Festival, Juneteenth programming, and Upper Darby Pride.
- Stakeholder presentation and program summary delivered to a county diversity, equity, and inclusion officer; a county council briefing and municipal pop-up logistics were coordinated.
- Program scale in the master resume: approximately 68.4 miles of trolley infrastructure and 20,000 daily riders.

## Skills demonstrated

Community engagement, stakeholder management, field operations, event staffing, public-sector communications, reporting, and team supervision.

## How clients benefit

Clients receive an outreach system that connects communications strategy to actual field execution: who will be reached, where, by whom, with what message, and how feedback returns to leadership.
