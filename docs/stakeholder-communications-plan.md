# Stakeholder Communications Plan

**Status:** Illustrative demonstration; stakeholder categories only, no real contact data.

| Audience | Primary concern | Core message | Channel | Owner | Timing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Program sponsor | Delivery confidence | Progress, risks, decisions needed | Briefing + dashboard | Program manager | Weekly |
| Municipal partners | Local impact and coordination | Schedule, locations, escalation path | Meeting + written summary | Stakeholder lead | Biweekly |
| Community organizations | Access and trust | How to participate and give feedback | Briefing + flyer | Outreach lead | Event-based |
| Riders and residents | Service impact | What is changing and where to get help | Pop-up + one-page summary | Field supervisor | Each deployment |
| Field team | Readiness | Assignment, message, safety, reporting | Huddle + packet | Field supervisor | Each deployment |

## Approval rule

Public dates, service impacts, and funding statements require confirmation from the accountable program source before release. Field staff should explain uncertainty plainly rather than speculate.

## Issue routing

- Program facts: subject-matter lead
- Scheduling or access: operations lead
- Media inquiry: designated spokesperson
- Safety concern: immediate escalation under site procedure
