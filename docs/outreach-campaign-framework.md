# Outreach Campaign Framework

**Status:** Illustrative demonstration based on verified field-program experience.

## 1. Define the public decision or behavior

State what the audience needs to know, decide, attend, complete, or provide feedback on.

## 2. Segment the audience

Group audiences by location, service use, language/access needs, stakeholder role, and likely concern. Do not treat "the public" as one audience.

## 3. Build the location plan

For each site, record expected foot traffic, access constraints, staffing, materials, partner contact role, and backup location. Confirm the site before public promotion.

## 4. Prepare the field packet

- Approved talking points
- One-page program summary
- Survey or feedback form
- Escalation path for technical or safety questions
- Activity log and photo/media rules
- Follow-up commitment log

## 5. Execute and capture

Staff record attendance, interactions, questions, feedback themes, accessibility issues, and commitments before leaving the site.

## 6. Report and follow up

Within the agreed reporting window, summarize reach, recurring themes, unresolved questions, and owner-assigned follow-ups. Close the loop with stakeholder partners.
