# Community Engagement Measurement Framework

**Status:** Illustrative demonstration.

## Measures that matter

| Dimension | Measure | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Reach | Locations, interactions, materials distributed | Shows whether the program was present where audiences are |
| Depth | Surveys, questions answered, partner meetings | Distinguishes visibility from meaningful engagement |
| Responsiveness | Follow-ups closed by due date | Tests whether feedback changes anything |
| Quality | Coding accuracy, complete logs, supervisor review | Protects credibility of reported themes |
| Equity of coverage | Distribution by geography and audience segment | Reveals missed neighborhoods or populations |
| Operations | Staffing fill rate, on-time setup, incidents | Shows whether delivery is sustainable |

## Reporting rule

Report outputs and outcomes separately. A large number of flyers distributed is an output; a resolved concern, completed referral, or documented change in understanding is closer to an outcome.
